# Board rules

The Teletype BBS is a small board run by a game collective. The rules are
the old ones, and they are short.

1. **Be excellent to each other.** No harassment, no hate, no flame wars.
2. **One name, one caller.** Call with a name, register it to keep it
   (You > Register…). Do not take somebody else's.
3. **Keep the wall friendly.** A note is for everyone in the clubhouse,
   and for the other boards of the network too. Answer the note you
   mean, not the writer.
4. **No warez, no spam, no ads.** Our files are our own games and tools,
   all free to download. Leave the cracked ones on the other boards.
5. **The sysop has the last word.** A note may be taken off the wall. A
   question or a complaint goes to Messages > Feedback (E on the C64).

A letter to the sysop tells our Discord only that it came, never what it
says.
