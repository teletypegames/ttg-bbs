# Board rules

The Teletype BBS is a small board run by a game collective. The rules are
the old ones, and they are short.

1. **Be excellent to each other.** No harassment, no hate, no flame wars.
2. **One name, one caller.** Call with a name, register it to keep it
   (Users > Register…). Do not take somebody else's.
3. **Post on topic.** General is for anything, Tech for code and hardware,
   Off-Topic for the rest. The Sysop board is the operator's notes.
4. **No warez, no spam, no ads.** Our files are our own games and tools,
   all free to download. Leave the cracked ones on the other boards.
5. **Private mail is private.** Do not quote a letter on a public board
   without its writer's leave.
6. **The sysop has the last word.** A question or a complaint goes to
   Messages > Feedback to the Sysop.

Board posts and sysop broadcasts are also sent to our Discord. Private mail
and feedback never are.
