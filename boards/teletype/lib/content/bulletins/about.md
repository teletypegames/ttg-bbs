# About Teletype Games

**Teletype Games** is an independent game development collective. We make
small retro-flavoured games on eight platforms, from the Commodore 64 and
the Plus/4 to TIC-80, Godot and the browser, and we build the road they
travel too: the pipeline that turns a push into a release, the catalog
where every title plays in the browser or downloads, and the stores that
carry it onto a retro box.

**This board** is our telnet clubhouse: the message boards, mail, the
oneliners wall, the live chat and the doors that live only here (SERMO,
the Commodore 64 and Plus/4, Chat of Ra, TTG Trivia).

**The rest of the network** has a board for each kind of thing:

- **WarpEngine BBS** (2329): our games and files, by ZMODEM or XMODEM
- **TTG Wiki BBS** (2325): the whole wiki, DOS corner included
- **TTG Blog BBS** (2328): our blog
- **Pixelia BBS** (2332): ANSI and PETSCII art
- **Starbase BBS** (2333): a Star Trek library, every episode and Memory Alpha
- **TTG Gate BBS** (2399): every board from one port

All of them are at teletypegames.org, and in a browser at
**https://bbsweb.teletypegames.org**.

**On the web:** the catalog at https://teletypegames.org, the wiki at
https://wiki.teletypegames.org, the source at https://git.teletypegames.org.
