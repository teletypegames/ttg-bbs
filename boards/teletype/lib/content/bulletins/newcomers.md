# New to BBSes?

A BBS, a *bulletin board system*, is what we called online before the
web: you dialed a board with your modem, one caller per phone line, read
the messages, played the doors, took the files home, and hung up so the
next caller could get in. This board answers on telnet instead of a
phone line, but it keeps the habits.

**Terminals.** Any telnet client works, but a BBS terminal does it best:
[SyncTERM](https://syncterm.bbsdev.net) and NetRunner show the CP437 art
and receive files by themselves. No client at all? The board runs in the
browser too: **https://bbsweb.teletypegames.org**.

**Getting around.** Alt and a letter opens a menu, F10 the first one.
Esc closes a window, Tab moves inside it, F5 makes it full screen. The
mouse works too.

**Files.** Our games and files have a board of their own, the
WarpEngine BBS on port 2329: it sends them to your terminal by ZMODEM.
Board > BBS Network lists every board of ours.

**Doors.** The Doors menu hands your line to another program: a
Commodore 64, the SERMO machine, a sun god. Each one tells how to come
back.

**The wall.** Messages > The Wall (F2) is the cork board of the
clubhouse: read the notes, pin your own, answer one. A word for the
sysop goes in Messages > Feedback (F7).

Still lost? Press **F1** and ask the TTG Master.
