# New to BBSes?

A BBS, a *bulletin board system*, is what we called online before the
web: you dialed a board with your modem, one caller per phone line, read
the messages, played the doors, took the files home, and hung up so the
next caller could get in. This board answers on telnet instead of a
phone line, but it keeps the habits.

**Getting around.** The cursor keys move the bar of a menu and RETURN
opens what it lights; the letter in front of an item opens it at once.
Lists are numbered: the cursor or the number typed, then RETURN. CRSR
left and right turn the pages, Q (or the ← key) goes back.

**Files.** Our games and files have a board of their own, the
WarpEngine BBS on port 2329: it sends them by XMODEM onto a C64 disk,
CCGMS takes them. N lists every board of ours.

**Doors.** R hands your line to another program: a Commodore 64, the
SERMO machine, a sun god. Each one tells how to come back.

**The wall.** M is the cork board of the clubhouse: read the notes, pin
your own, answer one. A word for the sysop goes in the mailbox, E.

Still lost? Press **T** and ask the TTG Master.
